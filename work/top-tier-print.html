Online store and order operations

Top Tier Print Company prints custom apparel. As Social Media Manager and Executive Assistant, I listed products, prepared customer invoices, tracked every order until delivery, and produced brand graphics.

Client
Top Tier Print Company
Period
2024 – June 2025
Role
Social Media Manager & Executive Assistant
Tools
Etsy, Google Sheets, Trello
Deliverables
Product listings · customer invoices · order tracking · mockups · brochure

Etsy product listings

I created product listings for the shop’s Etsy store, with photos, titles, and pricing for each design.

Customer invoices

Each order got an itemized invoice in Google Sheets: category, item, size, color, quantity, and price, then tax, fees, and balance due.

The customer’s name and the other invoice tabs are blurred for privacy.

Order tracking

I tracked orders in two places. In Trello, each order is a list that moves from pending through shipping to delivered. In Google Sheets, one row per order records confirmation, payment, status, dates, and the invoice number.

  • Monitored shipments and progress
  • Flagged delays, returns, and missing items

Customer names are blurred for privacy.

Brand graphics

For Top Tier Print Company I made brand layouts and merchandise mockups that show a logo on apparel and notebooks before printing, and designed a brochure.
