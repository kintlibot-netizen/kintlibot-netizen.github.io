About

I studied to teach social studies, and most of my work today supports a photography educator. It suits me. A lot of the job is making lessons clear: picking the right image, keeping a headline short, and checking that every link works before anything goes out.

I work remotely from Cagayan de Oro City in the Philippines. I like owning recurring work end to end, writing things down so the next person isn’t guessing, and getting a few options in front of a decision-maker quickly.
